# Host is always a Campus or Hub; Access Policy is separate from Delivery Mode

An Event's Host is always a Campus or a Hub, never a free-text club or department, so every Event has a known physical and institutional anchor for eligibility and gate rules. Who may attend (Access Policy, four values) and how they attend (Delivery Mode: in-person, online, hybrid) are two independent fields, replacing the earlier design that listed an online option as a fifth access tier. Merging them again would make a hybrid Event unable to also be campus-only.
