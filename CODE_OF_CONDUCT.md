# Code of Conduct

KamiKon adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 3.0**, as its code of conduct. The full text is at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/ and applies to every project space: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, project chats, and events where someone represents the project.

The Contributor Covenant is stewarded by the Organization for Ethical Source and licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Reporting an issue

Report conduct concerns by email to **pelmut2000@gmail.com**, with "KamiKon conduct" in the subject. Include what happened, where, when, and who was involved, with links where possible.

- Reports are acknowledged within 3 working days.
- Reports are kept confidential. Details are shared only with the people needed to address them, and never without the reporter's consent when the reporter can be identified.
- If the report concerns the person who receives these emails, say so, and the report will be handled by another maintainer once one is appointed (`GOVERNANCE.md`). Until then, you may contact GitHub directly through its reporting tools.

## Addressing and repairing harm

The project follows the remedies described in the Contributor Covenant 3.0 section "Addressing and Repairing Harm": warning, temporary limitation of participation, suspension, and permanent ban, chosen by the impact of the behaviour and whether it is repeated. Decisions and their reasons are recorded privately by the maintainers.

## Course cohorts

Students working on KamiKon as part of a course are also subject to their institution's own rules. A breach of this code may be reported to both.
